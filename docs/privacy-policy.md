# FlipCoin privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

FlipCoin is a coin-flipping app that shows a fun fact after every flip.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** FlipCoin does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or device data.

- The app has no account, sign-in, ads, analytics or crash reporting.
- The app does not request the internet permission and does not connect to the internet.
- All the facts are bundled inside the app.

## What stays on your device

The only thing the app saves is the list of fact topics you pick (for example "Space" or "Animals"),
so it remembers them next time. This is stored privately on your phone, is never sent anywhere,
and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

## Children

FlipCoin does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
