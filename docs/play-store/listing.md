## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Need to make a quick decision, or just want to learn something new? Flip the coin!

FlipCoin is a simple, cheerful coin toss with a twist: every flip reveals a fun fact
about a topic you love.

🐱 A COIN WITH A CAT
Our silver coin has a cute cat on both sides. Heads shows her face, and tails shows
... well, the other end, with a fluffy tail.

💡 A FUN FACT EVERY FLIP
Pick the topics you're curious about and get a new fact each time the coin lands:
• Animals
• Space
• Human Body
• Ocean
• History
• Food
• Science
• Cricket

Change your topics at any time from the Topics menu.

✨ SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• Unlimited flips, with a smooth flip animation
• A fair 50/50 toss every time
• No ads, no sign-in, no internet needed
• Collects no data at all

Settle a bet, decide who goes first, or just flip for the facts!
